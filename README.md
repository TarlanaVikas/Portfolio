## About

A personal portfolio showcasing my journey as a Computer Science and Engineering student, my technical skills, and the projects I build across full-stack development and AI/ML. Designed with a dark editorial aesthetic, subtle animated contour lines, and a responsive, accessible interface.

## Highlights

- **About & Education** — My background, academic journey, and interests.
- **Technical Skills** — Technologies, programming languages, and tools I work with.
- **Project Gallery** — AI/ML and full-stack projects with category filters, source code, and live demos.
- **Experience** — Professional experience and practical development work.
- **Certifications & Achievements** — Credentials, milestones, and accomplishments.
- **Contact** — Quick access to my email and professional profiles.
- **Resume** — Downloadable resume for a detailed overview of my experience and qualifications.

## Customization

Portfolio content is organized in `src/data.ts`, including projects, education, experience, skills, and certifications.

- **Projects:** Update project details, categories, source repositories, and live demo links.
- **Resume:** Replace `public/Vikas-Tarlana-Resume.pdf` while keeping the filename unchanged.
- **Visual design:** Customize colors, typography, and other design tokens in `src/styles.css`.
